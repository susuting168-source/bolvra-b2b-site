Installation Planning

Smart Lock Mortise Compatibility Checklist for Projects

Confirm the door and lock body details early to reduce installation delays, fit issues and avoidable after-sales questions.

BOLVRA Industry Blog · Updated August 2026

Key Takeaways

  • Record door and opening details before selecting a model.
  • Match the mortise, backset and latch direction to the installation.
  • Confirm the complete unlock-method combination, not only the exterior style.

1. Record the door material and thickness

Glass, aluminum, wood and metal doors can require different mounting structures and lock bodies. Record the door material and thickness for each project opening before selecting a product family.

2. Confirm opening direction and handing

Opening direction and handle handing can affect the installation plan. Share clear photos, measurements and opening information with the supplier so the proposed configuration matches the actual door.

3. Measure the mortise and backset

Mortise size, backset, latch position and related dimensions should be checked against the selected lock body. Do not assume that two locks with similar exterior panels use the same internal structure.

4. Match functions to the hardware

Fingerprint, passcode, IC card, app access and mechanical key backup can require different modules or structures. Confirm the required combination together with the lock body and door application.

5. Prepare installation information for the quotation

A clear project request can include door photos, dimensions, opening direction, lock body or mortise information, estimated quantity and the required access methods. This gives the supplier a better basis for model matching and sampling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which door details should be confirmed before choosing a smart lock?

Confirm door material, door thickness, opening direction, mortise size, backset, latch direction and the required unlock methods.

Why does mortise compatibility matter for project buyers?

Correct compatibility supports installation speed, door fit, product performance and a smoother after-sales process.
